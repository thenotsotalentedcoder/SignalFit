# SignalFit

**Find the work your business is built to win.**

SignalFit uses adaptive AI to understand your business and turn live market signals into client opportunities matched to your skills, capacity and goals.

This is the documentation-only pitch for **Build The Next Big Thing with AI - Devmarketplace x NED University**, October 2026. Product implementation is planned for the official build window, 19 October at 9:00 AM PKT through 22 October at 11:00 PM PKT.

Read [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) for the problem, complete user journey, adaptive AI workflow, MVP scope, data model, architecture, delivery plan and acceptance criteria.

## Planned experience

Sign up and enable 2FA, let adaptive AI learn your business, confirm your profile, research one sector and market, inspect source-backed opportunity matches, and save your shortlist for your next session.

## Planned stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase PostgreSQL/Auth, a hosted AI model and bounded web research. The exact providers and dependencies will be recorded during implementation.

## Current status

Pitch documentation only. There is no runnable product or deployment in this repository yet. Setup instructions, deployment URL, AI/dependency disclosure and links to two authored Devmarketplace code gigs will be added during the build.
